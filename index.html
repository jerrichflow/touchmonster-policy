Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

TouchMonster ("the Game") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the device, platform, permissions, and services enabled, the Game may collect or process:

  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted by applicable platform settings and user consent
  • Device information, including device model and operating system version
  • Gameplay progress, game settings, and usage data
  • Crash reports, diagnostic information, and error logs
  • Advertisement impressions and interaction data
  • Purchase transaction information provided by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store
  • Game account identifiers and game data required to save and restore progress

The Game does not require users to provide their real names, phone numbers, or postal addresses to play. However, information collected by third-party services may be subject to their respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Game
  • Save and restore gameplay progress
  • Improve stability, performance, and user experience
  • Diagnose errors and resolve technical issues
  • Analyze gameplay and service usage
  • Display advertisements and provide rewarded advertisement features
  • Process, verify, and restore in-app purchases where supported
  • Prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse of game services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

The Game may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase, including applicable database, authentication, analytics, and backend services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Services on Android
  • Apple App Store in-app purchase services on iOS

These third-party services may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4. Advertisements and Tracking

The Game may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and advertisement interaction information, depending on applicable laws, platform settings, and user permissions.

On iOS, permission will be requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework when tracking requiring such permission is performed. Users may manage applicable tracking permissions through their device settings.

On Android, users may manage applicable advertising privacy preferences through their device settings.

5. In-App Purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing on Android or Apple's App Store payment system on iOS.

The Game may receive transaction identifiers, product information, and purchase verification results necessary to deliver purchased content and prevent fraudulent transactions.

The Game does not directly collect or store users' full credit card numbers or payment card security codes. Payment information is handled by the relevant app store payment provider.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay data may be stored locally on the user's device and/or remotely using backend services such as Firebase.

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for game operation, security, service improvement, legal compliance, and other purposes described in this Policy. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service involved.

Users may contact the operator to request information about their personal data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of applicable data.

8. Data Security

The operator takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Game seeks to comply with applicable laws and platform requirements concerning children's privacy. Where required, appropriate consent or other protective measures will be applied.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, they may contact the operator to request review or deletion.

10. International Data Processing

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the user's country of residence through the infrastructure of third-party service providers. Applicable privacy and data protection requirements will be considered when such processing occurs.

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Game, third-party services, or applicable laws. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
